# Decisions and Assumptions

## Confirmed discovery decisions
- Product model: personal trainer management platform.
- Initial audience: independent personal trainers.
- Visual direction: modern athletic performance brand.
- Product name: PULSE.
- Delivery model: in-person and online.
- Nutrition tracking: included.
- Payments: basic payment tracking.
- Branding: temporary identity.
- Architecture: Next.js App Router, TypeScript and Tailwind demo with a companion Google Sheets-compatible tracker.

## Assumptions requiring validation
- One trainer is the primary user of the initial MVP.
- The trainer owns the client relationship and enters records.
- Nutrition is client-reported and informational only.
- Payment status is tracked manually.
- The companion workbook is an operational alternative, not a synchronised integration.
- SaaS pricing, subscription tiers and customer demand remain unvalidated.
