# PULSE — Client Demonstration Script

Suggested duration: 8–10 minutes.

## 1. Opening (1 minute)
“PULSE brings the everyday administration of an independent training business into one workspace: clients, programmes, sessions, nutrition notes and payment status.”

## 2. Dashboard (1 minute)
Show the active client count, today's schedule, received revenue and outstanding amount. Explain that the figures use fictional demo records.

## 3. Client workflow (1 minute)
Open Clients, search for a sample client, then add a fictional client. Explain the status field and client register.

## 4. Programme workflow (1 minute)
Open Programmes. Create a sample programme and assign it to a client.

## 5. Session workflow (1 minute)
Open Schedule. Create a session and mark a scheduled session completed.

## 6. Nutrition log (1 minute)
Add a client-reported meal with calories, protein and water. Explain that the module records information; it does not prescribe diets.

## 7. Payments (1 minute)
Record a fictional payment and mark it paid. Explicitly state that PULSE does not process transactions.

## 8. Companion tracker (1 minute)
Show the Google Sheets-compatible workbook and explain that it supports offline/manual workflows and can be imported into Google Sheets.

## 9. Discovery questions (1–2 minutes)
- How many active clients do you manage?
- How do you currently plan and update programmes?
- How are sessions booked, rescheduled and marked complete?
- What nutrition information do you actually need to record?
- How do you invoice and reconcile payments?
- Do clients need their own login or progress view?
- What reports would save you the most time?

## Close
Confirm the highest-value workflow, required integrations, user roles and production expectations before proposing a paid implementation.
