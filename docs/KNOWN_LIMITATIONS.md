# Known Limitations

- Demo records are fictional.
- Data persists only in the current browser using localStorage.
- No authentication or authorization.
- No multi-user or cross-device synchronisation.
- No production database.
- No client portal.
- No payment gateway or card processing.
- No invoicing or tax calculation.
- No automated email, SMS or WhatsApp notifications.
- No calendar provider integration.
- No medical assessment or dietary recommendation functionality.
- No production security, backup, monitoring or audit infrastructure.

Do not use the demo to store real personal, health, nutrition or payment information.
