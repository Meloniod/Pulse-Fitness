# PULSE — Product Brief

## Product
PULSE is a personal trainer management platform for independent trainers who deliver in-person and online coaching.

## Problem
Independent trainers often manage client information, training plans, session attendance, nutrition notes and payments across disconnected spreadsheets and messaging tools. This creates duplicated administration and makes it harder to maintain a clear view of client activity and cash flow.

## Target user
Independent personal trainers and small coaching practices.

## Value proposition
One workspace to organise client relationships, programmes, sessions, client-reported nutrition and payment status.

## Business model
Subscription SaaS is the intended future model. Pricing has not been validated and must not be represented as final.

## MVP modules
1. Overview dashboard
2. Client register
3. Programme planner
4. Session schedule
5. Nutrition log
6. Payment register
7. Performance summary

## Demo boundaries
The demo uses fictional records and browser-local persistence. It is not suitable for real client or health information and does not process payments.

## Success criteria for a client demonstration
- A trainer can understand the product in under two minutes.
- Core workflows can be demonstrated without external services.
- The companion tracker mirrors the app's operational modules.
- Known limitations and production steps are clearly disclosed.
