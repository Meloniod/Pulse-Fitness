# PULSE — Temporary Brand Notes

## Positioning
A focused operating workspace for independent personal trainers.

## Personality
Confident, disciplined, practical, energetic and supportive. Avoid exaggerated transformation claims or aggressive fitness clichés.

## Visual direction
- Primary surface: deep navy / charcoal
- Accent: athletic lime
- Supporting surfaces: clean off-white and light grey
- Typography: DM Sans for interface text; Manrope for display headings
- Layout: clear hierarchy, restrained borders, compact navigation and readable tables
- Responsive first; no glassmorphism, excessive gradients or decorative blobs

## Temporary identity
The PULSE name and visual treatment are placeholders for demonstration. Confirm trademark/domain availability and client brand requirements before commercial launch.
